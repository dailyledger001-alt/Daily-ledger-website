Yes. Free is $0 and needs no credit card. It includes 3 active tasks, 2 habits, 1 goal and 30 transactions per month. Basic workout, meal and grocery tracking is included, with current-month CSV export only. Email reminders, AI and advanced tools require Pro or Lifetime.

Pro is $19.99 / month, with 100 AI requests and 150 reminder emails per month. Lifetime is $99.99 one-time, with 60 AI requests and 300 reminder emails per month. Lifetime means the life of the Service. Compare all allowances.

Changing plans never deletes existing records. If you are over an allowance, you may need to get within it before adding more.

Active tasks, habits and goals count against your plan. Monthly transactions, reminder emails and AI requests reset each calendar month. Check Settings, Plan & usage for your current usage and confirmed allowances.

With Pro or Lifetime, describe what you need. The assistant proposes actions using your own account context. Nothing is saved until you confirm. Free does not include the assistant.

Free includes current-month CSV export only. Pro and Lifetime include historical reports and PDF export. Check export options in Settings, or contact support.
