How Daily Ledger protects passwords, sessions and the records in your account.

01

Passwords stay protected

Passwords are stored only as salted, memory-hard hashes, never plain text.

02

Sessions and request protection

Session cookies are HttpOnly and secure. No sign-in token is stored in local storage. Actions use CSRF protection.

03

Safer sign-in

Sign-in and verification attempts have rate limits and lockouts. Signup verification codes allow 5 attempts and expire after 5 minutes.

04

Account recovery

Password-reset links are single-use and short-lived.

05

Separate accounts

Data is isolated per account. Administrator access is limited to one controlled account with audit logging.

06

Hosting and payments

The app frontend runs on Vercel, with its API and PostgreSQL on Railway, over HTTPS. When payments launch, a third-party processor will handle card details; full card numbers do not touch our servers.
