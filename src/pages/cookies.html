What cookies are.

Small text files stored by your browser. Similar technologies (such as local storage) work in the same way.

What we use in the app (app.dailyledger.store)

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking in the app.

On the marketing website.

This marketing website does not use analytics or advertising tags and does not set non-essential cookies. Fonts are served locally; no third-party font requests or analytics scripts are loaded.

Your choices.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but the app will not work without the strictly necessary ones. Where required, a consent banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies on the marketing site.
