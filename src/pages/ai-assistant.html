Helpful, with clear limits.

The assistant can suggest actions within your own workspace. It cannot access someone else’s records, move money, guarantee results or replace a qualified professional.

AI suggestions are general guidance, not financial, medical or fitness advice. Check every proposal before confirming it.

Your requests and privacy

Your request text and the minimum account context needed to answer it are sent to the configured AI provider. Avoid adding sensitive information that is not needed for the request.

Provider details and retention settings must be confirmed before launch. Read the Privacy Policy →
